# Student Lifestyle & Academic Stress Analytics Dashboard

##  Executive Summary
This **Tableau Analytics Dashboard** provides a detailed examination of student well-being and academic performance metrics. It analyzes how key factors such as **Sleep Duration (SD)**, **Study Hours (SH)**, **Academic Pressure (AP)**, and **Financial Stress (FC)** directly correlate with **Student Count (SC)** and **Study Satisfaction (SS)** ratings[cite: 10].

---

##  Tools & Technologies Used
* **Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public:** Area charts, custom shape/bubble distributions, bar charts, and interactive tooltips[cite: 10].
* **Data Visualizations:** Multi-metric dashboard combining health, academic, and financial variables[cite: 10].

---

##  Dashboard Visualizations & Key Features

1. **Sleep Duration (SD) & Student Count (SC):**
   * Distribution of students across sleep buckets: **5-6 hours (123)**, **7-8 hours (128)**, **Less than 5 hours (123)**, and **More than 8 hours (128)**[cite: 10].
2. **Study Hours (SH) & Student Count (SC):**
   * Area chart tracking student density across daily study hours (0 to 12 hours), peaking at **10 hours (53 students)** and **8 hours (45 students)**[cite: 10].
3. **Study Satisfaction (SS) & Student Count (SC):**
   * Satisfaction rated on a 1–5 scale, with the highest concentration at **Rating 4 (116 students)** and **Rating 3 (103 students)**[cite: 10].
4. **Academic Pressure (AP) & Student Count (SC):**
   * Categorizes academic stress level impact on student counts (e.g., Level 3 pressure accounts for **125 students**, Level 4 for **92 students**)[cite: 10].
5. **Financial Stress (FC) & Student Count (SC):**
   * Evaluates financial burden across levels 1–5, with Level 1 impacting **110 students** and Level 2 affecting **102 students**[cite: 10].

## Key Findings & Insights
* **Optimal Study Hours:** A large group of students studies between **6 to 10 hours daily**, with a noticeable dip around 5 and 9 study hours[cite: 10].
* **Satisfaction Distribution:** The majority of students report moderate to high study satisfaction (Ratings 3 and 4)[cite: 10].
* **Pressure & Stress Impact:** Moderate academic pressure (AP Level 3) affects the largest cohort of students (125 students), highlighting the need for balanced workload management[cite: 10].
